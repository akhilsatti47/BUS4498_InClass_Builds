# Sports Bet Evaluation Rules

## Evidence Priority
Use information in this order:
1. Official league statistics and schedules
2. Official team injury reports and announcements
3. Recent player and team statistics
4. Reputable sports news reporting
5. Historical matchup information

Do not rely on unsupported social-media rumors when stronger sources are available.

## Player Props
Consider:
- Season average
- Recent-game performance
- Minutes or playing time
- Usage or opportunity
- Starting status
- Injuries
- Opponent defensive matchup
- Home or away performance when relevant
- Rest
- Expected game script

Do not judge a player prop using only the player's season average.

## Moneylines and Spreads
Consider:
- Team record
- Recent performance
- Injuries
- Starting lineup
- Home-field or home-court advantage
- Rest and travel
- Matchup strengths and weaknesses

## Parlays
Evaluate every leg individually.
Identify the leg with the greatest uncertainty.
Consider whether two or more legs are correlated.

## Confidence Ratings
1-3: Weak evidence or substantial uncertainty
4-5: Below-average confidence with meaningful risks
6: Moderate confidence
7: Solid evidence but meaningful risk remains
8: Strong evidence across several factors
9: Exceptionally strong evidence with relatively few identified risks
10: Avoid except in extremely unusual circumstances because sports outcomes remain uncertain

A confidence rating measures the strength of the available evidence, not certainty that a wager will win.

## Responsible Analysis
Never describe a wager as guaranteed, risk-free, safe money, a lock, or free money.
Never encourage chasing losses.
Clearly mention the strongest risk to the analysis.
